Privacy Policy

Meta muse — a Discord bot operated by Dylan2045ad. Effective October 2, 2026.

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes what information the Meta muse Discord bot can access and how it is used. The bot operates only inside Discord servers where the server owner has installed it.

2. Information the bot can access

Through the Discord API, in servers where it is installed, the bot can access:

  • Discord usernames, user IDs, and avatar information for server members;
  • Message content in channels the bot has permission to view (used for moderation and server-management features);
  • Member join and leave events (used for welcome messages and member counts);
  • Server structure information such as channel and role names and IDs.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to operate the bot's features: posting announcements and content updates, welcoming new members, and helping server staff with moderation (for example, removing spam or enforcing server rules). We do not sell personal information, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use Discord data for marketing targeting.

4. Storage and retention

The bot processes most information in real time without storing it. Minimal operational records may be kept to run the service — for example server and channel configuration, and moderation logs (such as a record that a message was removed or a member was timed out). These records are retained only as long as they are useful for running and moderating the server. Message content is not archived beyond what a moderation action itself records.

5. Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except (a) as part of the normal operation of the Discord platform and its API, or (b) when required by law.

6. Your choices

If you are a member of a server where the bot operates, you may contact the operator at the address below to ask what operational records relate to you or to request their removal. Removing the bot from a server stops all further processing for that server.

7. Children

The bot is intended for use within Discord, which requires users to be at least 13 years old, and it is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this URL.
