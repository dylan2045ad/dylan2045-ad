2 · Walkthrough — your first session

  • Ledger, row 1 (5 min). Pick the AI tool you’d miss most tomorrow. One row: tool, vendor, where its keys live, how you’d export, what replaces it.
  • Kill-switch card, line 1 (5 min). The owner’s name and the vendor’s revoke URL. Where do you set spend to zero? Who gets notified?
  • Permissions map (10 min). Who may spend, publish, delete, rotate keys — written down, with account names.
  • Fallback matrix (15 min). For that same dependency: primary, fallback, local/open-weight option, the trigger to switch, and how long switching should take.
  • Read the drill (10 min). The included filled Cursor/OpenAI access-cut drill, read as a story — then typed over with your own stack.
  • Weekly page (5 min, weekly). Continue / stop / start. The habit that keeps the plan a plan instead of archaeology.

Total first session: about 45 minutes — and the two highest-value pages take ten. These are manual planning sheets, not automated controls.
